WHO THIS IS FOR

SRM is for the person who is questioning, reforming and deliberately trying to understand who they are and decide who they will become.

  • Questioning identity — trying to understand who you are beyond the labels and expectations around you.
  • Reforming — recognising parts of yourself that need to change and deliberately working on them.
  • Rebuilding personality — becoming intentional about the traits, habits and ways of expressing yourself.
  • Creating character — taking responsibility for the kind of person you decide to become.
  • Structuring your value system — examining what you believe, what you value and what should guide your decisions.
  • Understanding yourself in relation to others — examining people, relationships, service and the way you show up in the lives of others.

You don't need to have it figured out before you start. You just need to be willing to examine yourself honestly.

WHY THIS PROGRAMME EXISTS

Here's why SRM exists, in FWC's own words.

At 11 or 12, I had it rough with life. My first attempt at suicide and season of feeling worthless happened during this period.

Think of that young me: an only son among girls, multiple health issues, a horrible character as judged by several, the typical weakling of a boy, academically dull. People's reactions and my school results didn't prove me wrong. How could they?

At 13, I moved to boarding school, and something began to shift — though there was still no clarity.

In that season, I found two lines that stayed with me:

"God who made you without you cannot save you without you."

"Everything you set out to achieve as a goal in your life sums up to you."

You're not a person made up of several individuals. You're a mixture of everything — your upbringing, your relationships, your traits, everything you've learned, consciously or not. So you are all your goals, regardless of the number. Seeing yourself that way is the salvation you need.

Every achievement reveals who you are. The process reveals the formation.

You aren't defined by what you achieve — your achievements are proof of who you are.

That's what self-realization is. Nothing outside you affects you until you let it in and let it become part of your makeup.

This programme is that process — built from someone who fought through it, for people ready to do the same.

SRM explores Self & Identity, Character & Mindset, Service, People & Relationships, Values & Deliberate Living, and God's love and Questions around it across 6 modules and 20+ topics.

Is there an age limit for SRM?
None. However, the keen focus of the enterprise is with teenagers & young adults. Exclusive sessions exist for senior citizens and most senior citizens who do not fall within afore age brackets
Is SRM a Christian mentorship programme?

Yes. Heart2Heart Enterprise is a Christian organization, and SRM is guided by Christocentric principles.

However, this is not a church scheme, neither is it in any way using teachings of church instead of professional counselling.

FWC is a graduate Psychologist, certified life coach and currently pursuing a Master's Degree in Clinical Psychology, so be rest assured to have professional, scientifically grounded knowledge guarded by Christocentric principles.

Our Christian identity informs the values and principles that guide our work, but we do not believe professional services should become a means of imposing religious dogma on people from differing backgrounds. You are therefore in a space where professionalism, respect and competence are taken seriously, while the work remains grounded in Christocentric principles.

Do I need to already know my purpose before joining SRM?

Not necessarily.

On SRM, purpose is understood slightly differently from the conventional meaning of the word.

The programme does not begin with the assumption that purpose simply means finding a career, discovering a grand calling, or identifying one specific thing you were born to do.

You will first engage with questions around your person, your character, your values, your relationships, your expression and the way you deliberately live.

Your understanding of purpose develops from there.

SRM is designed for individuals who are questioning their identity, reforming, rebuilding their personality, creating character, structuring their value system, and seeking to understand themselves in relation to others.
Is SRM simply about being motivated or finding quick answers?
No. SRM is a guided process of examination, learning, unlearning, reflection, application and greater self-understanding.
